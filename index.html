Frequently asked questions

What should I bring?

Wear closed-toe shoes and bring a water bottle. We provide gloves, tools, and lunch.

Is the garden accessible?

Yes. Paved paths connect the gate, beds, and picnic area, and we have seated tasks available.

Can I bring my kids?

Kids under 16 are welcome with an adult.
